Fuel, Joule, Rule

Fuel → Joule → Rule

Fuel is heterogeneous. Stranded gas, curtailed wind, nuclear baseload, hydro spill—no two are priced alike, and none of them is money. Fuel is where value is local.

Joule is the collapse. Combustion, fission, and photovoltaics all terminate in the same scalar, comparable across borders, technologies, and centuries. This is the step that makes fuel commensurable. But a joule is only a measurement; a claim to have spent one settles nothing, because no one else can check it.

Rule is what makes the spent joule a fact. The hash is the receipt and difficulty is the rule that reads it. Emission is not authorized, it is measured: Qi is released in proportion to work presented against a difficulty target, so the marginal cost of a unit is the marginal cost of the electricity that found it. No issuer, no promise, no redemption—a number anyone can verify.

The lineage closes backward. Because the rule prices in joules, and joules price fuel, a producer holding Qi holds a claim denominated in the same input they spend.

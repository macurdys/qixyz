Technical Thesis · 2026

Intelligence is a physical process.

Every inference dissipates energy and produces entropy. Qi makes that dissipation the reference frame — a unit of account measured, not declared.

Qi · Technical Thesis01
The Problem

It needs a true physical reference.

  • Intelligence is a chain of physical ratios. Each link is measurable, and the chain telescopes to one number:(ComputeJoule)×(InferenceCompute)×(IntelligenceInference)=IntelligenceJoule
  • We price the output, not the process. Tokens and inferences are billed; the thermodynamic work producing them is never measured.
  • Two identical joules are not comparable. The same computation in two countries settles at two prices for reasons the machine never sees.
Qi · Technical Thesis02
Foundation

One primitive: surprisal

A distinction defines the possibilities; the logarithm makes their probabilities additive. Everything that follows is a rotation of one object.

EventI(x)=−lnp(x)Surprisal. How unexpected one outcome is, in nats. Independent events add.
AverageH[p]=−∑ipilnpiEntropy is expected surprisal. For Ω equal states, H = ln Ω: counting made additive.
UnitsS=kBHBoltzmann’s constant converts nats to J/K. Same probabilities, physical dimensions.
Qi · Technical Thesis03
Equilibrium

Surprisal gets an energy scale

Maximize entropy subject to what is known and the answer is an exponential family. Constrain mean energy and it is the Boltzmann distribution.

Weightpi=e−βEiZBoltzmann distribution. β = 1/kBT is the multiplier that enforces mean energy.
GenerateF=−kBTlnZThe partition function Z generates free energy, mean energy, entropy, and fluctuations.
IdentityEi−F=kBT(−lnpi)Surprisal × kBT is energy. In the canonical ensemble, a probability distinction has a cost in joules.

Displacement

Divergence is Information Geometry

One quantity measures a system’s distance from equilibrium and a belief’s distance from the posterior.

CompareDKL(q∥p)=∑xqlnqp≥0Relative entropy. The expected difference of two surprisals; zero only when q = p.
PhysicsF[q]−Feq=kBT⋅DKL(q∥peq)Excess free energy is scaled divergence. Equilibrium is the minimum.
InferenceFV[q]=DKL(q∥P(h|d))−lnP(d)Variational free energy bounds the surprise of the data. Minimizing it is Bayesian inference.
Qi · Technical Thesis05
Mechanism

Proof of Entropy Minimization

PoEM turns surprisal into a running measurement, reading the full magnitude of every hash.

I(h)=−log2h2256 A hash with n leading zeros occurs with probability 1/2n — a receipt for n bits of surprisal.
  • Work becomes a continuous signal. Every hash carries information, not only the winners.
  • No shared clock. Events are ordered by causality; causal invariance keeps one consistent state.
  • Parallel by construction. Compute shards across chains; the entropy record stays additive.
Qi · Technical Thesis06
Arbitrage

Arbitraging Consensus

Bitcoinw(h)=1I(h)≥nThreshold. A hash counts only if it clears n leading zeros. Every near-miss is discarded.
Zcashw(h)=1I(h)≥nThreshold. Equihash is memory-hard, but the block still counts only if its hash clears the target.
PoEMw(h)=I(h)Continuous. Every leading zero and every fractional bit is measured. Nothing is thrown away.

The subsidy is paid on the cut, not the work. Measured to the fractional bit, the spread between what a threshold subsidy pays and what the work costs in joules becomes visible, and exploitable.

Consequence

What changes with a true reference

  • Cognition gets a cost function. The real price of a model is its dissipation — and it can finally be quoted.

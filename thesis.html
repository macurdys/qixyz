Technical Thesis

Energy Based Intelligence

01 / 08 — Premise

One Ledger For Work

Intelligence, money, and economics are the same accounting problem

Every act of computation is a physical process. It dissipates energy, produces entropy, and leaves a record. Modern economies price the outputs of that process — tokens, inferences, GPU-hours — while systematically failing to price the process itself.

The thesis of Qi is simple: the thermodynamic cost of work is the only unit of account that does not require a trusted issuer. Once you can measure it, you can settle in it.

◈

Intelligence

Inference is dissipation. Model capability has a floor set by physics, not by capital.

◆

Money

A unit whose issuance cost is a measured quantity of joules cannot be printed into existence.

◉

Economics

Prices become measurements. Value flows along thermodynamic gradients, not narrative ones.

02 / 08 — The Measurement Problem

Why Fiat Cannot Price Compute

The unit of account is the bug

Compute markets today are denominated in currencies whose supply is a policy variable. That introduces a term into every cost calculation that has nothing to do with the underlying physics of the machine.

  • Energy is local, currency is global. A kilowatt-hour in Iceland and one in Virginia are physically identical and financially incommensurable.
  • Depreciation schedules are fiction. Hardware amortization is an accounting convention layered over a real dissipation process.
  • Contracts settle in claims, not in work. Every compute contract is ultimately a promise about a balance sheet, not a receipt for joules spent.

The consequence is that the fastest-growing sector in the economy — inference — has no native unit. It borrows one, and inherits every distortion that comes with it.

03 / 08 — Foundation

Information Is Physical

Shannon → Landauer → Settlement

The bridge between information theory and thermodynamics is not a metaphor. Erasing a bit of information has a minimum energy cost at temperature T:

Emin = kB · T · ln 2 Landauer's bound — the thermodynamic floor of irreversible computation

Real machines run many orders of magnitude above this bound, but the bound is what makes the accounting well-posed: there exists a physically grounded lower limit on the cost of any computation, and every real implementation reports its distance from that limit.

That distance is measurable, comparable across hardware and geography, and impossible to fake without actually doing the work.

04 / 08 — Mechanism

Proof of Entropy Minima

PoEM — work as a continuous, not binary, signal

Conventional proof-of-work treats a hash as pass/fail against a threshold. This discards information: a hash that beats the target by a wide margin represents more expected work than one that barely clears it.

PoEM reads the full magnitude of each solution and treats it as an entropy measurement. The chain's canonical history is the one that represents the minimum cumulative entropy — the trajectory that most efficiently converts energy into ordered state.

ΔS ∝ −log₂( h / 2256 ) Entropy contribution of a solution h — continuous, not thresholded
  • Faster convergence. Fork choice resolves on a continuous signal rather than a discrete count.
  • Better use of expended work. No hash below the threshold is wasted information.
  • Sharding-compatible. Entropy is additive across parallel chains in a way that block-count is not.
05 / 08 — The Token

Qi As An Energy Unit

A stable claim on joules, not on a balance sheet

Qi is issued as a function of measured entropy production. Its issuance schedule is not a chosen parameter — it is a readout of how much work the network actually performed.

  • No discretionary issuance. There is no committee. There is a measurement.
  • Denominated in work, not in belief. One Qi corresponds to a defined quantity of computational dissipation.
  • Naturally counter-cyclical against energy prices. When energy is cheap, more work is affordable; the unit tracks the resource, not the sentiment.

This is what makes Qi usable as an accounting layer for compute rather than merely as a speculative asset. A datacenter can quote in it. A model provider can settle in it. Neither has to trust the other's currency assumptions.

06 / 08 — Architecture

The Settlement Stack

From joules to invoices
  • Layer 0 — Physical. Generation and compute co-located. Solar, curtailed grid capacity, waste heat recovery.
  • Layer 1 — Consensus. PoEM-secured chain producing a verifiable entropy record.
  • Layer 2 — Unit. Qi issuance bound to that record; the numéraire for everything above.
  • Layer 3 — Markets. Compute futures, capacity auctions, inference metering, settlement rails.
  • Layer 4 — Applications. Model providers and agents that price their own operation in energy terms.

Each layer is only as trustworthy as the measurement beneath it — which is precisely the point. The stack is designed so that the deepest layer is the one that requires the least trust.

07 / 08 — Consequence

Energy Based Economics

What changes when the unit changes

Give an economy a unit of account grounded in physics and a set of second-order effects follow, none of which require anyone to adopt a new ideology.

  • Stranded energy becomes bankable. Curtailed generation converts directly into a liquid unit at the point of production.
  • Efficiency becomes revenue. Approaching the Landauer bound is not a sustainability initiative; it is margin.
  • Cross-border settlement stops needing an intermediary currency. Joules are already fungible.
  • AI capex gets an honest denominator. The real cost of a model is its dissipation, and it can finally be quoted.
08 / 08 — Status

Where This Stands

Open questions and honest limits

This is a research programme with working components, not a finished system. The parts that are load-bearing and still contested:

  • Measurement fidelity. Entropy accounting is exact at the protocol layer and approximate at the hardware layer. Closing that gap is open work.
  • Volatility of the underlying. Energy prices move. A unit that tracks them faithfully will move with them — that is a feature for accounting and a friction for retail use.
  • Adoption is a coordination problem. A unit of account is worth what others will quote in. Physics does not solve that; markets do.

The claim being made here is narrower than it may sound: not that energy money is inevitable, but that compute is the first market large enough, and physical enough, for the accounting to actually pay for itself.
